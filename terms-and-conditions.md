## 7. The Trading Process

### 7.1 How a Trade Works

1. A match is identified between two users
2. Both users review the proposed swap — sticker lists are shown, but names and addresses are not revealed at this stage
3. Both users accept the match
4. Payment is confirmed where a fee applies (otherwise this step is skipped)
5. Postal addresses are shared by the platform with both parties
6. Each party posts their stickers to the other
7. Each party marks the trade as received when their stickers arrive
8. If no confirmation is received within 14 days of the sender marking as sent, the trade is auto-confirmed as complete

### 7.2 Postal Trades Only (Tier 3)

All Tier 3 trades are conducted by post. Face-to-face exchanges with strangers are not supported or encouraged. We strongly advise against arranging to meet in person with any user you have been matched with through Tier 3.

### 7.3 Auto-Confirmation

If a recipient does not confirm or dispute receipt within 14 days of the sender marking a trade as sent, the trade is automatically confirmed as complete. Both parties receive an email reminder before the auto-confirmation triggers. Auto-confirmation does not prevent you from reporting a no-show — see Section 9.

### 7.4 Your Responsibilities as a Sender

When you agree to a trade you commit to:

- Dispatching your stickers within a reasonable time (we recommend within 5 working days of accepting the match)
- Sending the correct stickers as agreed
- Taking reasonable care in packaging to avoid damage in transit
- Marking the trade as sent in your account once dispatched

### 7.5 Your Responsibilities as a Recipient

When you receive stickers you commit to:

- Confirming receipt promptly in your account
- Raising any dispute within the 14-day window, not after auto-confirmation
- Acting in good faith when reporting issues

## 9. Reporting a No-Show

### 9.1 What is a No-Show

A no-show occurs when you have accepted a trade, the other party has confirmed they sent their stickers, but you have not received anything within a reasonable time.

### 9.2 How to Report

You may report a no-show through your trade record. Reports can be submitted after the 14-day window has elapsed without receipt. You should not report a no-show while the 14-day window is still open.

### 9.3 Consequences for the Reported Party

A confirmed no-show results in a strike being recorded against the sender's account. If the sender has uploaded video proof of posting prior to the report, a reduced penalty may apply.

Accumulation of strikes may result in temporary suspension of matching privileges. The thresholds and durations are set out in Section 10.

### 9.4 Goodwill Credits

When a no-show is confirmed against you as the recipient, goodwill match credits are issued to your account automatically. These credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred or withdrawn. They may be used to access additional Tier 3 matches when a paid or allowance model is active.

### 9.5 Your Responsibility as a Reporter

Reports must be made honestly and in good faith. The platform tracks reporting behaviour in the same way it tracks sending behaviour. Users who are found to make repeated reports against well-regarded members of the community may have penalties applied to their own account. See Section 10.3.

By submitting a no-show report you confirm that, to the best of your knowledge, the stickers you agreed to receive have not arrived and you have waited a reasonable period.

## 10. Reputation and Account Standing

### 10.1 Reputation Score

Every user has a reputation score calculated automatically from their trading history. The score is used to determine matching priority and eligibility. The exact calculation is proprietary and is not disclosed. Users are informed of their current account standing through their profile page.

A higher reputation score improves the quality and frequency of matches you are offered. A lower score reduces matching priority. Users with suspended accounts are not eligible for Tier 3 matching during the suspension period.

### 10.2 Strikes — Senders

A strike is a negative mark applied to your account when a no-show report is confirmed against you.

The following thresholds apply:

| Strikes accumulated | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1 | Noted on account, no immediate action |
| 2 | Noted on account, reduced matching priority |
| 3 | Account suspended from Tier 3 matching for 1 month |
| 3 (second suspension) | Suspended for 2 months |
| 3 (third suspension) | Suspended for 4 months |
| Each subsequent | Suspension duration doubles |

On return from suspension, your strike count resets. Continued good trading behaviour progressively improves your standing over time.

### 10.3 Strikes — Reporters

Reporting behaviour is subject to the same standards as sending behaviour. If you repeatedly report users who are in good standing with the platform community, penalties may be applied to your account.

The same strike thresholds and suspension durations set out in Section 10.2 apply to reporters. Goodwill credits are not issued for trades where you are found to have reported in bad faith.

### 10.4 Improving Your Standing

Good trading behaviour improves your account standing over time. Completing trades honestly, on time, and without disputes contributes positively to your reputation. Negative marks do not remain on your account indefinitely — a consistent record of successful trades will progressively restore your standing, regardless of past issues.

### 10.5 Suspension

During a suspension you may not access Tier 3 matching. You may continue to use Tier 1 and Tier 2. You may continue to manage your collection. Existing accepted trades must still be completed — suspension does not release you from a commitment already made.

On return from suspension your account is reinstated automatically. No action is required from you.

### 10.6 Permanent Restrictions

We reserve the right to permanently restrict or close an account in cases of serious, repeated, or deliberate misuse of the platform. This includes but is not limited to:

- Repeated confirmed no-shows showing a pattern of deliberate non-sending
- Repeated bad-faith reporting showing a pattern of deliberate abuse
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent a suspension
- Any behaviour that causes harm to other users or to the platform community

Permanent restrictions are applied at our sole discretion. If you believe a permanent restriction has been applied in error, you may contact us at [contact email].
