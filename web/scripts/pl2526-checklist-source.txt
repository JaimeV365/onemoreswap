From the Topps website:

A New Era, built on heritage. The Topps Premier League sticker collection has been completely reimagined in 2026 for the modern fan while staying true to the tradition that made it legendary. Inside the 96-page album, every Premier League club is celebrated across detailed squad pages and feature spreads. From the building blocks of base stickers, to the legendary Hall of Fame subset, this collection appeals to audiences of all ages. The Classic Combo stickers feature legendary partnerships from Premier League history. Whilst the elusive Cloud 9 stickers add an exciting chase to every rip. And for those who love to dig into the data, the New Groundbreakers subset pays tribute to Premier League record holders, both past and present. With 561 stickers to collect, this collection offers variety, rarity and nostalgia in a way that’s fun and achievable to complete. Designed for today’s fans, this set will capture the colour, history, and excitement of the Premier League, whilst catering to the appeal of the modern collector. Alongside classic paper stickers are brand-new holographic finishes, colourful parallels, and numbered chases from #/50 down to incredibly rare 1/1 Foilfractors. Sticker collecting holds such a powerful place in British football culture. This is the set to collect that pays homage to the past, and innovates for the future.

When you find your Kick Off Sticker Card in the Starter Pack you need to peel off the gold foil Premier League Logo sticker to reveal the player. If you are a one-club collector it would be worthwhile getting two of your club's sticker card, one to leave intact and the other to peel and show the player. Luckily, the cards are number so it will be easy to find two copies of the one you want.
