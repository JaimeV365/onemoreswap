Design principles

1. Platform ≠ album

Yellow and red are One More Swap. Purple is Premier League accent only. World Cup / Euro / other leagues get their own album tokens without repainting the whole app.

2. Light by default

Day-to-day collection and matching UI uses warm light neutrals. Full purple backgrounds are tiring and too PL-coded for a multi-album product.

3. Dark mode is optional

Dark theme uses grey body type (#C5C0C8), not white — pairs better with yellow badges and PL-tinted surfaces.

4. Collector-native name

One More Swap is the phrase every parent hears at the kitchen table. Lean into completion energy — never alternate readings, never cheeky asides in UI.

5. Yellow fills, red outlines

Yellow is the only loud fill (CTAs, spares). Platform red is crimson borders and text — never pink/red backgrounds. Panini-bright red is album-scoped only.

6. Wordmark = plain text

One More Swap in Russo One. Hero: Just need one more?
